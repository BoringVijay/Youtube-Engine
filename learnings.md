# Learnings

Rules our own videos taught us. feedback-loop writes here. Overrides generic advice.

## Rules
- Animation over slideshows. A video that reads as frames stitched together is a fail, however pretty the frames. The bar is the lab's paper-cut ocean shot (crane down, data pulsing through cables). Source: Vijay's note on the gold cube cut. 2026-11-07
- Never repeat a frame or a composition. The gold cube cut showed the cube on the same baseball field in about 15 of 24 shots and held identical frames under several captions. Source: Vijay's note + review sheet. 2026-11-07
- One number per fact. The gold cube cut showed 197,037 and 216,000 tonnes for the same total. Source: Vijay's note + review sheet. 2026-11-07
- The penguin is cast in every story (protagonist, sidekick, villain, antagonist, NPC or cameo by topic), not only a hidden cameo. Source: Vijay, 2026-11-07

## Hunches (n < 3)
- The gold cube story felt flat and the voice felt off to Vijay. Cause not yet pinned down: check hook strength, stakes and the narrator choice (run the voice blind test) on the next two videos. 2026-11-07
