# Genre research, 14 Nov 2026 (Curious Shots)

Full write-up: Claude doc "Curious Shots: genre research and topic opportunities". This file keeps the raw measurements so topic-scout can reuse them.

## Thesis
True stories told in the internet's fiction formats. Famous weird stories are saturated (10M+ Shorts already exist); generic fiction what-ifs are drowning in AI uploads. The gap: real stories in the formats that are rising ("In this world...", second-person choices, "100 days until", exploration ladders, two-sentence stories).

## Scores are never blended
Demand (evidence people watch it or its nearest sibling), Whitespace (biggest existing Short and count of 1M+ Shorts in the top 25 search results), Viral (hook, comment and replay triggers), Fit (style, budget, tone). Qualify only if Demand >= 6 and Whitespace >= 6.
