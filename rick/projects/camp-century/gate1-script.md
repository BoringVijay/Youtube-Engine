# The City the Ice Ate: gate 1 (hooks and script)

Facts (verified, with sources in the repo research note):
- **Construction:** Camp Century was built from June 1959 to October 1960, about 30 ft under the Greenland ice.
- **Facilities:** a chapel, a barbershop, a theater, a library and a 10-bed infirmary. A portable nuclear reactor (PM-2A) supplied the power.
- **People:** up to about 200 soldiers. The base mascot was a husky named Mukluk.
- **Upkeep:** crews trimmed more than 120 tons of snow and ice from the tunnels every month.
- **Project Iceworm:**
  - The secret plan was 600 "Iceman" missiles on trains under the ice, 4 miles apart, with about 11,000 troops.
  - It was kept secret from Denmark.
  - It was cancelled in 1963 because the ice kept moving and squeezed the tracks.
- **The end:** the reactor came out in 1964 and the camp was abandoned by 1966-67.
- **The waste:** sewage, fuel, PCBs and low-level radioactive coolant were left behind on the assumption the snow would bury them forever.
- **The return:** a 2016 study found that as early as 2090, melting at the site could overtake snowfall.

### Critic notes on version A
- **Strong:**
  - An absurd hook on frame 1.
  - A doubter with a meme-able line ("you're just growing").
  - Escalation in physical, readable steps: ceiling → barber kneeling → 120 tons → rails bending.
  - The wait-what, then a real mechanism with an everyday analogy in its own set.
  - A spectacle payoff on the last word, and a callback (the mug).
- **Watch:**
  - The Denmark line must stay factual and light: the joke is the pastry, not the politics.
  - Keep the 2090 claim as "as early as" (that's what the study says).
- **Risk:** two reveals (the missiles, then the waste). Mitigation: the missiles are one quick beat, and the waste is the payoff.
- **Score:** 9.0/10. Hold a final rating until it's voiced and timed in the animatic.
